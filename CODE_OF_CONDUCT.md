# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.
Participants must communicate respectfully, accept constructive feedback, and
focus on what is best for users and the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and
other abusive behavior are unacceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may edit
or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participants whose
conduct is inappropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security/contact channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with
confidentiality appropriate to the circumstances.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
